Kestrel Bay opens new Avonmouth warehouse and creates 12 local jobs

Bristol, 9 February 2026 - Bristol outdoor brand Kestrel Bay Supplies has opened a
2,300 square metre warehouse at Portway Trade Park in Avonmouth, more than tripling its
storage space and creating 12 new jobs in the area.

The new site replaces two smaller units in St Philips and brings all of the company's
stock, online order picking and trade deliveries under one roof. It can hold up to
4,200 pallets and ship up to 3,500 parcels a day at peak season.

Kestrel Bay designs tents, rucksacks, sleeping bags and camp kitchen kit for UK
weather. It sells online, through two online marketplaces and to more than 60 outdoor
retailers across the UK and Ireland. The company has grown from 6 to 40 people since it
was founded in 2018.

Helen Marsh, Chief Executive and co-founder of Kestrel Bay, said:
"We started the business in a garage in Totterdown with one tent design and a lot of
optimism. Moving into Avonmouth means we can get kit to customers faster, stock deeper
ranges for our retail partners and keep growing in Bristol. We are especially pleased
to be hiring locally, and we have already filled 8 of the 12 new roles."

The warehouse has been fitted with energy-efficient LED lighting, rooftop solar panels
that supply around 40% of the site's electricity, and a cardboard baler so that all
incoming packaging can be recycled. Outgoing orders are packed in recyclable paper
packaging with no plastic void fill.

Kestrel Bay is currently recruiting for warehouse operatives and a warehouse team
leader. Details are at kestrelbay.example/careers.
