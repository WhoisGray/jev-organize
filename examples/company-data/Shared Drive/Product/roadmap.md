# Product and digital roadmap - H2 2026 to H1 2027

Updated 28 July 2026 by Tom Albright (Product Director). Confidential - internal planning, dates can move.

## Guiding goals

1. Grow average order value by adding a lightweight backpacking range.
2. Cut returns caused by product faults to under 2% of units.
3. Replace the web shop platform before peak 2027.
