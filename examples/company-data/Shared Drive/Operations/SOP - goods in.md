# SOP WH-03: Goods in (receiving supplier deliveries)

Site: Unit 4, Portway Trade Park, Avonmouth
Version 2.1 · Issued: 3 March 2026 · Owner: Dave Kinsella, Warehouse Manager
Applies to: all warehouse staff and agency workers on the goods-in bay

## Purpose

Make sure every supplier delivery is checked, booked into the warehouse system (WMS) and put away correctly, so stock figures are accurate and supplier invoices can be matched to what we actually received.

## Before the delivery

1. Suppliers must book a slot at least 24 hours ahead on 0117 496 0735. Goods-in hours are 07:30-15:00, Monday to Friday.
2. The goods-in lead prints the expected-deliveries list from the WMS at 07:15 each morning.
3. Check the purchase order (PO) is open in the WMS. No PO = do not unload. Call the Purchasing Coordinator (Frankie Lowe, ext. 735).

## Unloading and checking

4. Wear safety boots and a hi-vis vest. Only trained staff may use the forklift or pallet truck.
5. Check the vehicle seal and the number of pallets against the delivery note before signing anything.
6. Unload to the quarantine lane (yellow floor markings), never straight to racking.
7. Count cartons against the delivery note. Sign "unchecked" if the driver cannot wait for a full count.
8. Open 1 in 10 cartons (at least 2 per SKU) and check product, colour, size and quantity per carton.
9. For fabric rolls, check the roll labels show PO number, fabric code and batch, and that a certificate of conformity is attached for each batch.
10. Photograph any damage and note it on the delivery note before the driver leaves.

## Booking in

11. Scan each pallet label and receive against the PO line in the WMS. Enter the actual quantity, not the ordered quantity.
12. If there is a difference of more than 2% or any damage, create a discrepancy record (DR) and email purchasing@kestrelbay.example with photos.
13. Scan the signed delivery note and save it to the PO record the same day.

## Put-away

14. Print put-away labels. Move stock to the suggested bin, scan the bin, then scan the product.
15. Samples, returns to supplier and quarantined goods go to the QC cage, never to pick faces.

## End of day

16. The goods-in lead checks that no pallets are left in the quarantine lane without a DR.
17. Report the day's receipts and any discrepancies in the 15:30 shift handover.
