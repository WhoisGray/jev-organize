# Data Retention Policy

**Kestrel Bay Supplies Ltd** · Policy LEG-POL-03 · Version 2.0
Approved: 14 January 2026 (Helen Marsh, CEO, for the Board) · Next review: January 2028
Policy owner: Sarah Whitlock (Data Protection Lead) · Legal adviser review: completed

## 1. Why we have this policy

We must keep some records for legal, tax and contractual reasons, and we must not keep personal data for longer than we need it. This policy sets how long we keep each type of record and how we delete it. It applies to paper and electronic records in every location, including the shared drive, email, the web shop, the warehouse system and personal desktops and downloads folders.

## 2. Retention schedule

| Record type | Examples | Keep for | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounting records | invoices, receipts, bank statements, VAT returns | 6 years after the end of the financial year | Finance |
| Payroll records | payslips, PAYE, pension contributions | 6 years after the end of the tax year | Finance |
| Employee files | contracts, reviews, absence records | 6 years after employment ends | People team |
| Unsuccessful candidates | CVs, interview notes | 6 months after the role is filled | People team |
| Right to work checks | passport copies | 2 years after employment ends | People team |
| Contracts with suppliers and customers | supply, logistics, NDAs, leases | 6 years after expiry (12 years if signed as a deed) | Legal / FD |
| Customer orders | order history, delivery details | 6 years after the order | Operations |
| Marketing consent | email sign-ups, preferences | until consent is withdrawn, or 2 years with no engagement | Marketing |
| Support tickets | emails, chat logs, call notes | 3 years after closure | Customer Support |
| Server and access logs | web server, VPN, firewall | 90 days | IT |
| Backups | nightly system backups | 30 days | IT |
| CCTV (Avonmouth) | camera recordings | 30 days unless needed for an incident | Operations |

## 3. Deletion

- Records are deleted or anonymised at the end of the retention period, at the latest during the quarterly clean-up.
- Paper records are shredded on site using the locked confidential waste bins.
- Copies in personal folders (Desktop, Downloads, "old stuff" and similar) must be deleted or moved to the correct shared drive folder. Duplicates must be removed.

## 4. Legal holds

If there is a dispute, claim or investigation, the Finance Director may place a legal hold. Records under a hold must not be deleted, even if the retention period has passed.

## 5. Responsibilities

- Record owners in the table above make sure their records follow this schedule.
- IT runs automatic deletion for logs and backups.
- The Data Protection Lead checks compliance once a year and reports to the Board.
