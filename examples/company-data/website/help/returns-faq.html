Returns, refunds and guarantees

Last updated: 4 August 2026

How long do I have to return something?

You can return any item within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. Tents and sleeping bags can be returned even if you have used them once, as long as they are clean and dry and packed with all their parts.

How do I start a return?

  1. Go to My orders and choose the item you want to return. Guest customers can use the returns form with their order number (it starts with KB-) and postcode.
  2. Choose a reason and print the free returns label, or choose a courier collection from your home for £4.20.
  3. Pack the item securely. You can reuse our box.
  4. Drop it at any parcel shop within 14 days of printing the label.

When will I get my refund?

We refund to your original payment method within 5 working days of the return reaching our warehouse in Avonmouth. You will get an email when it has been processed. Your bank may take a further 3-5 days to show it.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes. Choose "exchange" when you start your return and pick the new size or colour. We send the replacement as soon as the original is scanned at the parcel shop.

What does the guarantee cover?

  • All products: 2 years against faults in materials and manufacture.
  • Tent poles: 5 years. If a pole section breaks, we send a replacement section or a full pole free of charge.
  • The guarantee does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, or fire and heat damage.

My tent pole has snapped. What should I do?

Send us a photo of the break and the batch number printed on the pole bag to help@kestrelbay.example. We normally dispatch a replacement pole within 2 working days. You do not need to send the whole tent back.

Trade and wholesale returns

Retail partners should contact their account manager. Trade returns follow the terms of your trading agreement.
