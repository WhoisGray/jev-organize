Warehouse Team Leader

  • Location: Avonmouth, Bristol (on site)
  • Salary: £30,000 - £32,500 a year, plus 8% shift allowance on late shifts
  • Hours: 40 hours a week, alternating early (06:00-14:30) and late (13:30-22:00) weeks
  • Contract: permanent, full time
  • Posted: 3 August 2026 · Closing date: 14 August 2026

About us

Kestrel Bay designs tents, rucksacks and camping kit for British weather and sells it online and through more than 60 outdoor retailers. Earlier this year we moved into a new 2,300 m² warehouse at Portway Trade Park, Avonmouth, and we ship up to 3,500 parcels a day in peak season.

The role

You will lead a shift team of 6-10 warehouse operatives (more in peak) and make sure every order goes out accurately and on time. You will report to the Warehouse Manager.

  • Plan and run your shift: picking, packing, goods in, returns and trade pallets.
  • Hit daily dispatch targets (98% of orders out the same or next day).
  • Train new starters and temporary staff, and run shift briefings.
  • Keep the site safe: toolbox talks, forklift checks, near-miss reporting.
  • Work with Customer Support on urgent orders and with Purchasing on deliveries.
  • Run cycle counts and fix stock discrepancies in the warehouse system.

About you

  • At least 2 years' experience supervising a team in a warehouse or distribution centre.
  • A valid counterbalance forklift licence (reach truck is a bonus).
  • Confident with handheld scanners and a warehouse management system.
  • Calm under pressure, especially from October to December.
  • An interest in the outdoors is nice to have, but not required.

What you get

  • 25 days' holiday plus bank holidays
  • 5% employer pension contribution
  • 30% staff discount and a £150 annual kit allowance
  • Free parking and a cycle-to-work scheme

How to apply

Send your CV and a short note on why you would like the job to careers@kestrelbay.example by 14 August 2026. Interviews will be held at Avonmouth on 18 and 19 August.

We welcome applications from everyone. If you need any adjustments for your interview, just tell us.
