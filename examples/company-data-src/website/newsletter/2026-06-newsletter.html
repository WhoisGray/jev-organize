Camp kitchen: smoky chickpea and chorizo one-pot

Serves 2, one pan, 15 minutes on the Emberlite 2. Fry 60 g diced chorizo, add a tin of chickpeas, a tin of chopped tomatoes and a teaspoon of smoked paprika. Simmer for 8 minutes and finish with a handful of spinach. Mop up with flatbreads.

Pick the right pitch

  • Avoid dips and the bottom of slopes - cold air and rainwater both collect there.
  • Pitch with the narrow end of your tent into the wind.
  • Check above you for dead branches before you peg out.
  • Leave no trace: take everything home, including food scraps.
