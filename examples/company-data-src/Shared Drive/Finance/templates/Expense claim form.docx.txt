# Categories
Travel (rail, bus, taxi, parking) / Accommodation / Subsistence (meals when away overnight, maximum £30 per day) / Trade shows and events / Stationery and small equipment under £100 / Other (please explain).
# Rules
Submit your claim within 30 days of the spend. Attach an itemised receipt for every item over £10; card slips alone are not accepted. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Claims over £500 need Finance Director approval. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.
