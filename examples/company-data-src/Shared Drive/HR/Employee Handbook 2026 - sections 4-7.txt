KESTREL BAY SUPPLIES LTD - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK 2026
Excerpt: sections 4 to 7
Version 4.1, issued 1 February 2026 by the People team. This handbook is not part of
your contract unless a section says so.

4. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
4.1 Office staff work 37.5 hours a week, normally 09:00-17:30 with an unpaid lunch
    break of one hour. Core hours are 10:00-16:00.
4.2 Warehouse staff work shift patterns agreed with the Warehouse Manager. Shift rotas
    are published at least two weeks in advance.
4.3 If you will be late or absent, call your manager (not just a text or chat
    message) before your start time.
4.4 Hybrid working: after probation, office staff may work from home up to two days
    a week if their role allows. Tuesdays are office days for everyone.

5. HOLIDAYS
5.1 The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
5.2 Full-time staff get 25 days plus bank holidays, rising by one day for each full
    year of service after two years, up to a maximum of 28 days.
5.3 Book holiday in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for up to 5 days, and
    four weeks ahead for longer periods.
5.4 Warehouse and support staff cannot take holiday between 1 November and
    23 December except in exceptional cases agreed by the Operations & People Manager.
5.5 You must keep 3 days for the Christmas shutdown.
5.6 Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to 31 March of the next year.

6. SICKNESS ABSENCE
6.1 Tell your manager by phone before your start time on the first day of absence.
6.2 For absences of up to 7 calendar days, complete a self-certification form on
    your return. For longer absences, you need a fit note from your doctor.
6.3 Your manager will hold a short return-to-work meeting after every absence.
6.4 Company sick pay: after probation, full pay for up to 10 working days in any
    rolling 12 months; after that, Statutory Sick Pay only.
6.5 We may ask for an occupational health referral for long-term or repeated
    absence. Medical information is kept by the People team only.

7. EXPENSES
7.1 We reimburse reasonable costs you incur on company business.
7.2 Use the Expense Claim Form (FIN-07) in the Finance templates folder. Claims must
    be submitted within 30 days with itemised receipts.
7.3 Book rail travel in standard class at least 7 days ahead where possible. Hotel
    stays are capped at 110 GBP a night (150 GBP in London).
7.4 Mileage is paid at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles.
7.5 Company credit cards are held by the Finance Director and the Head of Sales
    only. Card details must never be emailed or saved in documents.

For sections 8-12 (conduct, disciplinary, grievance, whistleblowing, leaving)
see the full handbook on the shared drive.
