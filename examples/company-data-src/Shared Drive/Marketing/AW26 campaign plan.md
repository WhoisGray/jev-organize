# AW26 campaign plan - "Into the Fells"

Version 1.3 · 3 June 2026 · Owner: Ellie Fairbrother (Marketing Manager)
Status: approved in principle by Helen Marsh; budget to Board on 16 July
Confidential - do not share outside Kestrel Bay before launch

## 1. Objectives (10 September to 30 November 2026)

- Online (DTC) revenue of £640,000 in the campaign period (+14% on the same period in 2025)
- 9,000 new Base Camp newsletter subscribers
- 25% of campaign revenue from new customers
- Support wholesale sell-in: campaign assets supplied to Brightwater, Cairn & Co and 20 independent retailers

## 2. Audience

- Primary: "weekend wanderers", aged 28-45, living in cities, doing 4-8 camping trips a year, mostly in the Lake District, Peak District, Snowdonia and Dartmoor.
- Secondary: families moving from festival and campsite camping to wild camping.

## 3. Message

"Into the Fells" - autumn is the best time to be out there, and our kit is built for it. Proof points: 5-year pole guarantee, Fellwalker 3 storm-tested at 60 mph, recycled fills.

## 5. Timeline

- 12-15 August: shoot
- 31 August: teaser on social
- 7 September: early access email to Base Camp members
- 10 September: launch, all channels
- 17-18 September: Outdoor Trade Expo, stand B14
- October: "first wild camp" customer stories, layering guide
- 1-30 November: Black Friday period - no deeper than 25% off, core range excluded

## 6. KPIs and reporting

Weekly dashboard (Sam Adeyemi): revenue by channel, cost per new customer (target under £18), return on ad spend (target 4.5), email sign-ups, sell-through at partner retailers.

## 7. Risks

- Ridgeback 2P not ready for campaign: do not feature it; teaser only in 2027.
- Stock: Tarn 45L slate had stock-outs in June. Frankie Lowe to confirm stock cover of at least 8 weeks for hero products by 1 September.
- Weather: a very wet September helps waterproofs but hurts tents - keep budget flexible between product groups.
