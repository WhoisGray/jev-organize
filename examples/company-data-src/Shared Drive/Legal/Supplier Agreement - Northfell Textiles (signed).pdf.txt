SUPPLY AGREEMENT - TECHNICAL FABRICS                           Ref: KBS/NTL/2026

BETWEEN
(1) Northfell Textiles Ltd, a company registered in England (no. 04417826) whose
    registered office is at Mill 3, Calder Bank Road, Halifax HX1 4RT ("Supplier"); and
(2) Kestrel Bay Supplies Ltd, a company registered in England (no. 11482730) whose
    registered office is at 14 Quayside Lane, Bristol BS1 6XJ ("Customer").

BACKGROUND
A. The parties have traded under a letter of terms dated 14 June 2023, which
   expires on 31 March 2026.
B. The Customer wishes to secure supply of coated technical fabrics for its tent
   and outerwear ranges on the terms below.

1. TERM
1.1 This Agreement starts on 1 April 2026 (the "Commencement Date") and ends on
    31 March 2028 unless terminated earlier under clause 9.
1.2 Either party may propose a renewal by written notice no later than
    30 September 2027.

2. PRODUCTS, PRICES AND VOLUMES
2.1 The Supplier will supply the fabrics in Schedule 1 at the prices in Schedule 2.
    Prices are fixed until 31 March 2027.
2.2 From 1 April 2027 the Supplier may increase prices once, by no more than
    UK CPI plus 1%, on 60 days' written notice.
2.3 The Customer will order at least 18,000 linear metres per contract year. If it
    orders less, it will pay a shortfall fee of 0.40 GBP per metre.
3. ORDERS AND DELIVERY
3.1 Orders are placed by purchase order. Standard lead time is 21 days.
3.2 Delivery is DAP Unit 4, Portway Trade Park, Avonmouth, Bristol BS11 9QD.
3.3 Each batch is supplied with a certificate of conformity.
4. QUALITY
4.1 Fabrics must meet Schedule 1, including hydrostatic head (flysheet minimum
    3,000 mm; groundsheet minimum 5,000 mm).
4.2 The Customer may reject non-conforming goods within 14 days of delivery. The
    Supplier will replace them within 10 working days at its own cost.
5. PAYMENT
5.1 The Customer will pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of invoice date.
6. EXCLUSIVITY
6.1 During the term the Supplier will not sell the "Kestrel olive" colourway
    (code NT-RS70-OL) to any other outdoor retailer in the UK or Ireland.
7. LIABILITY
7.1 Each party's total liability in a contract year is capped at the fees paid or
    payable in that year, except for death, personal injury or fraud.
8. CONFIDENTIALITY
8.1 Each party will keep the other's pricing, volumes and designs confidential
    during the term and for three years after it ends.
9. TERMINATION
9.1 Either party may terminate on written notice if the other commits a material
    breach not remedied within 30 days, or becomes insolvent.
10. GOVERNING LAW - the law of England and Wales.
