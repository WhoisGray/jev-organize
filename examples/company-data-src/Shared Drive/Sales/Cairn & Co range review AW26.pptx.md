Kestrel Bay x Cairn & Co Outfitters - AW26 range review
- Presented at Cairn & Co head office, Keswick, 9 June 2026
- James Corrigan, Ben Hollis and Lukas Brandt (Kestrel Bay Supplies Ltd)
- Confidential - shared under the mutual NDA of February 2026
---
Our partnership so far
- Trading since 2023; stocked in all 14 Cairn & Co stores
- 2025 purchases £148,600 at trade prices (+23% on 2024)
- Full-price sell-through 71% on AW25 (category average 58%)
- Top lines: Fellwalker 3 tent, Tarn 45L rucksack, Kestrel Lite -5 sleeping bag
---
AW25 performance by line
- Fellwalker 3: 212 units sold, 4% returns, sold out in October
- Tarn 45L slate: 388 units, strongest in Keswick and Ambleside stores
- Emberlite 2 stove: 610 units, often bought with gas bundles
- Stormshell jacket: 41% sell-through, too many size L and XL - fix the size curve
---
Recommended AW26 range for Cairn & Co
- Keep: Fellwalker 3, Tarn 45L, Kestrel Lite -5, Emberlite 2
- Add: Ridgeback 2P (from February 2027), Driftwood camp chair
- Reduce: Stormshell jacket to 6 core sizes per store
- Suggested opening order £61,800 at trade, delivered in 3 drops (August, September, October)
---
Own-label concept - "Cairn Trail by Kestrel Bay"
- 2 tents (2-person and 4-person) and 2 sleeping bags in Cairn & Co colours
- Designed by our team, made in our existing factories, exclusive to Cairn & Co
- Minimum order 600 units per style per season
- First delivery for spring/summer 2027
---
Pricing and margin (own-label)
- Target retailer margin 52% at recommended retail prices
- Trade prices held for 12 months from first order
- 2% settlement discount for payment within 14 days
- Volume tier B discount (8%) continues on branded lines
