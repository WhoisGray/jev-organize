# 1. Summary
Brightwater Retail Group runs 38 stores and a growing online business. We propose a core range of 8 lines across tents, rucksacks, sleeping bags, cooking, outerwear and camp furniture, delivered to your national distribution centre from 1 September 2026, with replenishment every two weeks until February 2027.
Initial order value at the trade prices below: £122,170 excluding VAT, plus a pre-order of 150 Ridgeback 2P tents (£19,200) for delivery in February 2027.

# 3. Commercial terms
Payment terms: 45 days end of month. A 2% settlement discount applies to invoices paid within 14 days.
Marketing contribution: 2% of net purchases, for in-store point of sale and one joint email campaign in October.
Minimum order for free delivery: £2,500 per drop.
Delivery: 5 working days from order to your distribution centre, with ASN and pallet labels to your specification.
# 4. What Brightwater gets
An exclusive "Kestrel olive" colourway on the Fellwalker 3 for the high street.
Staff training for store teams: 6 regional sessions in August, run by our product team.
Warranty handled by us directly: 2 years on all products, 5 years on tent poles.
Monthly sell-through reporting and a named account manager (Ben Hollis).

1. Brightwater to confirm the range by 12 June 2026.
2. Supplier set-up forms and trading agreement signed by 30 June 2026.
