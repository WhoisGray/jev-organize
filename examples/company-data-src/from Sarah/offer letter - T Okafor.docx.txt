# Offer of employment - Warehouse Team Leader
Dear Tobi,
Following your interview with Dave Kinsella and me on 19 August, I am delighted to offer you the position of Warehouse Team Leader at Kestrel Bay Supplies Ltd. You will be based at our warehouse at Unit 4, Portway Trade Park, Avonmouth, Bristol BS11 9QD, and will report to Dave Kinsella, Warehouse Manager.
# Main terms of the offer
Start date: Monday 5 October 2026, or another date we agree.
Salary: £31,500 a year, paid monthly in arrears.
Hours: 40 hours a week on alternating early (06:00-14:30) and late (13:30-22:00) shift weeks. A shift allowance of 8% is paid for late-shift weeks.
Holiday: 25 days a year plus bank holidays, pro rata in your first holiday year.
Probation: 6 months.
Pension: auto-enrolment, 5% employer and 3% employee contributions.
Benefits: 30% staff discount, cycle-to-work scheme, free on-site parking and an annual kit allowance of £150.

This offer depends on:
1. proof of your right to work in the UK (please bring your passport on your first day);
2. two satisfactory references, one of them from your current employer;
3. a copy of your counterbalance forklift licence, which must be valid on your start date.
