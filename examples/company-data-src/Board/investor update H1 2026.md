# Kestrel Bay - investor update, first half 2026

From: Helen Marsh, Chief Executive · 24 July 2026
Confidential - for shareholders only, please do not forward

Dear all,

Here is our update for the six months to 30 June 2026. The short version: we are on plan, the new warehouse is paying off, and autumn looks strong. The Board reviewed these numbers on 16 July.

## Numbers

| | H1 2026 actual | H1 2026 budget | H1 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | £2,537,900 | £2,520,000 | £2,108,400 |
| Gross margin | 53.0% | 53.0% | 51.6% |
| EBITDA | £10,500 | £9,800 | -£41,300 |
| Cash at 30 June | £286,900 | £240,000 | £198,700 |

Revenue grew 20% year on year. As usual the first quarter was loss-making (EBITDA -£104,000) and the second quarter made it back (EBITDA +£114,500). The £400,000 overdraft was not used.

## What went well

- **Avonmouth warehouse.** We moved in February. Pick rates are up almost 50% and we can now handle 3,500 parcels a day at peak.
- **Supply.** We signed a two-year fabric agreement with our main mill, with prices fixed until March 2027 and an exclusive colourway.
- **Wholesale.** A 38-store high-street chain has listed our AW26 range. The first order (£122,170) arrived on 14 July.
- **Online.** The new checkout cut abandonment from 71% to 63%.

## What did not go well

- **Tent poles.** A batch of Fellwalker 3 poles from March has failed more than it should. We have quarantined the remaining stock, are refunding affected customers in full, and will switch to a stronger 9.5 mm pole by the end of September.
- **Carrier performance** was below the agreed service level in May and June. The new logistics contract from 1 July has lower rates and service credits.
- **Security.** One mailbox was compromised by a phishing email in May. There was no financial loss, and we have tightened MFA and blocked email forwarding.

## Outlook

We are keeping the full-year budget of £5.75m revenue and £242,750 EBITDA. The AW26 "Into the Fells" campaign launches on 10 September, and the Ridgeback 2P lightweight tent launches on 1 February 2027.

## How you can help

1. Introductions to garden centre and leisure chains (we have a strong range for them).
2. Recommendations for an experienced interim operations lead for the October-December peak.

Thank you for your continued support. Questions are welcome at any time.

Helen
