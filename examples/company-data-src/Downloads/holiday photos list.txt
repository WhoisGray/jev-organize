LOGISTICS SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract no. OLL-KBS-2026-07

This Agreement is made on 16 June 2026

BETWEEN
Oakridge Logistics Ltd, company number 05583170, of Oakridge House, Fairway Distribution
Park, Swindon SN3 4TZ ("Oakridge"), and
Kestrel Bay Supplies Ltd, company number 11482730, of 14 Quayside Lane, Bristol BS1 6XJ
("the Client").

1. SERVICES
1.1 Oakridge will collect parcels and pallets from the Client's warehouse at Unit 4,
Portway Trade Park, Avonmouth, Bristol BS11 9QD, Monday to Friday at 16:30 (and at
12:00 on Saturdays from 1 November to 23 December), and deliver them to addresses in
mainland UK, Northern Ireland, the Scottish Highlands and islands.
1.2 Standard service is next working day for parcels collected by 16:30 to mainland UK.
1.3 Oakridge will provide tracking links to end customers by email and a one-hour
delivery window on the morning of delivery.

2. TERM
2.1 This Agreement starts on 1 July 2026 and continues for 12 months. It then renews
automatically for further 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days' notice.

3. RATES (excluding VAT and fuel surcharge)
Parcel up to 2 kg ........................ 3.45 GBP
Parcel 2-15 kg ............................ 4.95 GBP
Parcel 15-30 kg ........................... 7.80 GBP
Two-person pallet delivery (up to 500 kg) . 58.00 GBP
Highlands, islands and Northern Ireland ... surcharge of 6.50 GBP per parcel
Returns collection from the end customer .. 4.20 GBP
Fuel surcharge: reviewed monthly against the published diesel index; capped at 6%.
Rates are fixed until 30 June 2027.

4. SERVICE LEVELS
4.1 On-time delivery target: 98.5% of parcels each calendar month.
4.2 If Oakridge misses the target, it will credit 5% of that month's charges for each
full percentage point below target, up to a maximum of 20%.
4.3 Lost or damaged parcels are compensated at cost value up to 250 GBP per parcel,
if the claim is made within 14 days of the expected delivery date.

5. PEAK SEASON
5.1 From 1 October to 23 December Oakridge guarantees capacity of up to 3,500 parcels a
day, provided the Client gives a weekly forecast every Monday.

6. PAYMENT
6.1 Oakridge invoices monthly in arrears. The Client pays by Direct Debit 30 days after
the invoice date.

7. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY
7.1 Oakridge maintains goods in transit insurance of at least 250,000 GBP and public
liability insurance of at least 5,000,000 GBP.
7.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

8. DATA PROTECTION
8.1 Oakridge processes end-customer names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses
only to deliver parcels, as a processor for the Client, and deletes them 90 days after
delivery.

9. GOVERNING LAW
England and Wales.
